# Wonderlands — play guide / 操作說明

Choose a terrain below the ocean, then click/tap or drag to paint. Land, Hill, Mountain, River, and Village are available immediately. Ocean erases. There is no cost and no required sequence.

Neighbors join automatically. Removing a tile also changes its neighbors. Try drawing a familiar island silhouette, connecting a river to a mountain, or grouping settlements. BEEP notices discoveries; you never need to accept an assignment. Earned discoveries remain in the journal even if you change the map. Unknown recipes are hidden until discovered.

Use Inspect, then select a placed cell to choose an available appearance. Auto follows the landscape again. Mountain/hill orientations and earned river variants are cosmetic; settlement options depend on their current surroundings. The city needs both a qualifying settlement block and supporting land, and can revert if either is removed.

Undo and Redo are below the palette. One drag is one edit. History is bounded to 60 operations, belongs to the current session and region, and resets on region switch, import, or reload. Clear requires confirmation and can be undone. It preserves discoveries and the other region.

Zoom in with + for precise touch placement, then use the board's scrollbars to pan. Zoom out to return to the overview. To scroll the page on a phone, drag outside the board. Board gestures paint; pinch zoom is not used as a map control.

Keyboard: focus the board with Tab, then use arrows to select a cell. Space/Enter paints. Keys 1–6 choose tools; I toggles Inspect. Control/Command Z undoes; Shift adds redo. Escape closes dialogs.

Autosave stores both regions, achievements, settings, and appearance overrides on this browser and site address. Settings provides a backup export and a validated import with replacement confirmation. Export before clearing browser data or moving to another site. If storage is blocked/full, the game remains editable and displays a warning; export a backup before leaving.

EN / 繁 switches language and saves your preference. Settings includes your optional name, BEEP ideas, mute, reduced motion, and cell-guide controls. Audio starts only after a user gesture and is optional. Explore at your own pace; a new region may find you along the way.

## 繁體中文

在海洋下方選擇工具，再點選或拖曳繪製。平原、丘陵、山脈、河流與村落一開始就能自由使用；海洋工具可以擦除。沒有費用，也沒有必須遵守的順序。

相鄰地形會自動連接，移除地形也會改變周圍的外觀。試著畫出熟悉的島嶼、讓河流接近山脈，或讓村落聚在一起。BEEP 會注意到新發現，但不會要求你接受任務。已獲得的發現永久保留，未知配方不會預先揭露。

點選「檢視地形」後選取格子，可以選擇目前相容的外觀。「自動」會重新依照周圍環境選擇外觀。城市需要符合條件的村落區塊與相鄰陸地；移除條件後會恢復成其他聚落樣貌。

工具列下方可復原或重做，一次拖曳算一次操作，最多保留 60 次。歷史紀錄只屬於目前工作階段與區域，重新載入、切換區域或匯入存檔時會清除。「清空區域」需要確認，且可以復原；發現與另一個區域都會保留。

使用 + 放大後，可以透過地圖捲動條平移，縮小則回到全貌。手機上請在地圖外滑動以捲動網頁；地圖內的手勢用來繪製，不使用雙指縮放控制地圖。

鍵盤：以 Tab 聚焦地圖後，用方向鍵選取格子；空白鍵或 Enter 放置地形；1 至 6 選擇工具；I 切換檢視；Control／Command Z 復原，加 Shift 重做；Escape 關閉對話框。

進度會自動儲存於目前瀏覽器與網站位址，包含兩個區域、發現、設定與外觀選擇。設定中可匯出備份或匯入經驗證的存檔；取代目前進度前會要求確認。清除瀏覽器資料或更換網站前，請先匯出。若瀏覽器儲存空間無法使用，遊戲仍可編輯並顯示警告，離開前務必匯出備份。

EN / 繁 可切換語言。設定中還有稱呼、BEEP 靈感、靜音、減少動態效果與格線選項。依自己的步調探索，也許會遇見新的區域。
